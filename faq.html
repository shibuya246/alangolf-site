AlanGolf FAQ

Who is AlanGolf?

AlanGolf is a golfer chasing the all-time hole-in-one record on Pebble Beach Hole 7 on Toptracer. Every ace and every session is on video on the YouTube channel @alangolfing.

How many hole-in-ones does AlanGolf have?

20 hole-in-ones on Pebble Beach Hole 7 (87 yards), all on Toptracer with a 60° Vokey wedge. 16 of them are on video and 4 early ones have photos.

What is AlanGolf's world rank?

#7 in the world on the all-time Pebble Beach Hole 7 leaderboard on Toptracer, ranked by number of hole-in-ones.

What club are the aces hit with?

Every one is with the same club: a 60° Vokey wedge from 87 yards.

What was the best session?

Three hole-in-ones in 51 minutes on 16 October 2026: aces #16, #17 and #18.

Is this real golf or a simulator?

It's real shots with a real ball at a driving range. Toptracer cameras track the ball and the screen shows where it would finish on the virtual Pebble Beach Hole 7.

Why show the near misses too?

Because that's what chasing an ace really looks like. Each session video shows every shot from that day in order, near misses and aces, so you can see how close the others came.

How can I play Pebble Beach Hole 7 on Toptracer?

Find a Toptracer range, get the free Toptracer Range app, scan the QR code on the bay screen, then choose Closest to the Pin and Pebble Beach Golf Links Hole 7. See the What is Toptracer page for the full steps.

How often are new videos posted?

After every session. Aces get their own Short, and each day gets one session video with every shot.

Is AlanGolf affiliated with Toptracer or Titleist?

No. AlanGolf is independent. Leaderboard information comes from Toptracer Range.
